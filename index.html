Process

How an engagement runs

  1. Intro email. You tell me about your brand, platforms, and goals.
  2. Scope and quote. I confirm the scope and the final quote (USD and PKR) before any work starts.
  3. Payment. Starter Sprints are 100% upfront. Project work is 50% upfront / 50% on delivery.
  4. Kickoff. You send brand basics, access, and raw footage as needed.
  5. Production. I build the calendar and posts, or edit the videos. Any AI assistance is labelled, and every cut is human-reviewed.
  6. Delivery and revisions. You review, and revisions follow the terms in the quote.
  7. Ongoing. If we continue, I run the monthly content rhythm and a monthly report.

FAQ

Honest answers

Why are there no client testimonials?

I have no paid clients yet, and I won't invent any. The proof here is my own work, which is labelled as mine and can be checked on the live profiles.

How is AI used in editing?

AI assists with the workflow, such as rough cuts and caption drafts. A person (me) reviews and corrects every cut before delivery, and I label AI assistance wherever editing is mentioned.

What are typical timelines?

Timelines depend on scope, so each quote states a delivery date before you pay. I'd rather confirm a realistic date than promise a fixed turnaround up front.

How do revisions work?

The quote states how many revision rounds are included, agreed before work starts. Requests outside that scope are quoted separately.

What are the payment terms?

Starter Sprints are 100% upfront. Project work is 50% upfront / 50% on delivery. Ongoing work follows the monthly terms in your quote.

What do you need from a client to start?

A short brief about your brand and goals, access to your accounts (or a way to publish), any raw footage or assets, brand guidelines if you have them, and one person who approves work.
